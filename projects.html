Propulsion & Test

Solid-motor development and the ground infrastructure built to characterise it.

001

Static Fire Test Stand

Horizontal test stand in its assembled state: a timber pallet base carrying twin linear rails, with a hardboard blast screen behind it bearing the club roundel. Motors clamp to a sliding carriage so thrust is transferred along the rails rather than into the frame.

  • Propulsion
  • Ground Support
002

Thrust Carriage

The sliding carriage in close view. The motor casing is held in aluminium clamps riding on two polished rods, which keep the thrust axis true and hold the load path purely axial through the run.

  • Propulsion
  • Mechanism
003

Stand Fabrication

Assembling the carriage by hand under a work light. Nearly all of the stand’s structure is measured, cut, drilled and fastened in-house rather than bought in.

  • Fabrication
  • In-House
004

Test Campaign Departure

The team with the stand crated and loaded before a winter firing. Test windows are scheduled after dark, when the site is clear and ambient conditions are stable.

  • Operations
  • Field
005

Pre-Test Review

Reviewing the instrumentation on a laptop in the field at first light, shortly before a firing. Dense fog is normal at the site through the winter campaign season.

  • Telemetry

Avionics & Electronics

Flight computers, radio links and the bench work behind them.

006

Flight Computer Prototype

Breadboard build of a flight computer — an Arduino Uno paired with a barometric pressure module for altitude and a nine-axis inertial measurement unit for attitude. The bench stage validates the sensor stack before the design is committed to a printed board.

  • Avionics
  • Prototype
007

Radio Control and Power

A six-channel FS-CT6B transmitter with its paired receiver, a 2200 mAh three-cell lithium-polymer pack and a propeller: the standard control and power set carried by the club’s fixed-wing airframes.

  • Flight Control
  • Hardware
008

Hands-On Build

An evening working session, members gathered around a wiring harness. Build sessions run informally through the term and are open to anyone who turns up.

Design & Simulation

Geometry and structural analysis carried out before anything is cut.

016

Nozzle Structural Study

A factor-of-safety plot from a static structural study of a converging–diverging nozzle in SolidWorks Simulation, run to locate the limiting section of the geometry before manufacture. Results at this iteration sit well below unity, marking it as a working step rather than a final part.
