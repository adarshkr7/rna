/ 06 — Timeline

Progress

2018

Inception

August 15, 2018

The Rocketry & Aviation Club was established to advance student-led aerospace research and innovation at IIT Patna.

2021

Avionics

September 05, 2021

Designed and developed custom flight computers and telemetry systems to enable reliable real-time data acquisition during flight operations.

2022

Propulsion

April 18, 2022

Successfully conducted the first static firing of a solid rocket motor, meeting predefined thrust and burn duration parameters.

2025

Launch

April 18, 2025

Introduced “Taara,” the club’s first flight vehicle, representing a significant milestone in integrated design, testing, and launch capabilities.

2026

Innovation

January 16, 2026

Completed the fourth static fire test using in-house KNDX propellant and advanced thermal insulation, achieving an I-class motor performance of 480 Ns total impulse.
